## Full description
Prepare for HEC's USAT (Undergraduate Studies Admission Test) with full mock tests built exactly to HEC's published content weightages, for all six categories.

FULL MOCK TESTS, THE REAL PATTERN
• 20 complete mocks for each category: USAT-E, USAT-M, USAT-CS, USAT-GS, USAT-COM and USAT-A
• Part I: 75 MCQs in 100 minutes: Verbal Reasoning 20, Quantitative Reasoning 25 and your 30-question subject block
• Part II: choose one argument and one narrative essay prompt from three each, 40 minutes
• No calculator, flags, a question map and free movement between questions, like the paper test

SCORED, THEN EXPLAINED
• Marks out of 100 against the qualifying score of 50
• Results by section and your weakest topics
• Every question explained: the working, the rule, and why the tempting option is wrong
• Rate your essays against marking points and a model answer

STUDY EVERY TOPIC
• Study notes with worked examples and traps for every topic: Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Accounting, Commerce, Islamiat, Ethics, Pakistan Studies and General Knowledge
• Practice sets separate from the mocks, with instant answers
• Essay practice with model answers

MEMORY TRICKS AND GUIDANCE
• 300+ memory cards: formula sheets, tables and mnemonics
• How to memorise: active recall, spaced repetition, mnemonics
• Registration steps, test-day rules and checklist, OMR technique, time plans, study plans and common mistakes

Free, works offline, no account. Your results stay on your phone.

Prep Simulator for USAT is independent practice material by HomiLabs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Higher Education Commission (HEC) or its Education Testing Council (ETC). All questions, passages and essay prompts are original, not past papers.
