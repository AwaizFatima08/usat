# USAT: practical guide facts (registration, fees, eligibility, test day, acceptance, prep-app market). Researched 17 Nov 2026

Scope: HEC Education Testing Council (ETC) Undergraduate Studies Admission Test, 2025-2026. Most third-party sites (ilmkidunya, eduvision, pakadmissions, blogs) copy each other and often mix up years. Official HEC/ETC pages are marked **[official]**. ETC's portal (etc.hec.gov.pk) is a login-based app and could not be read directly.

## 1. Eligibility, registration, fee, roll-number slips, dates and frequency

### Takeaway
USAT is open to HSSC/equivalent (12 years) graduates and to candidates who have sat their final Intermediate/A-level exams and are waiting for results. Registration is online only, at etc.hec.gov.pk, with the fee paid through 1Link/1Bill. In 2026 ETC ran 5 USAT sessions (official calendar), plus a special session for the Hungary scholarship. No official page gave the fee amount: blogs say Rs 2,000, older figures are Rs 1,200 and Rs 1,750. Treat the fee as unverified.

### Cited Findings
**Eligibility**
- Open to candidates who completed HSSC or equivalent (Grade 12), and to those who sat final exams and are awaiting results — [ProPakistani, 19 Aug 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/08/19/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2025/)
- Dec 2026 cycle: HSSC (FA, FSc, ICS, ICom) or an equivalent 12-year qualification, including candidates awaiting results — [ilmkidunya, Nov 2026](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22)
- Eduvision explicitly includes students appearing in final Intermediate **or A-Level** exams — [Eduvision](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/hec-usat-2026-dates-syllabus-and-how-to-apply-news-3613)

**Categories (the candidate picks one by stream; it cannot be changed after submission)**
- **[official]** USAT has six categories. Candidates choose the one matching their academic stream, and the category cannot be changed after submission — [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)
- The six categories: USAT-E (Pre-Engineering), USAT-M (Pre-Medical), USAT-CS (Computer Science), USAT-COM (Commerce), USAT-GS (General Science), USAT-A (Arts and Humanities). Applicants should check which category their intended programme accepts — [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22)
- HEC publishes an official content-weightage PDF for all streams — [HEC: Content Weightages for USAT (PDF)](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf). Not opened here; pattern research covers it.

**Registration process [official, HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)**
- Candidates create a profile on the ETC portal with their own valid CNIC. Those under 18 use the NADRA Juvenile Card / B-Form number.
- The name must match the Matric certificate. The profile is locked once registration is complete.
- Duplicate profiles are not allowed, including ones made with a relative's CNIC.
- The latest test link appears on the candidate's dashboard.
- The test city is chosen from a list and cannot be changed after registration.
- Payment: under "My Application", click "Online Payment" to generate a Consumer Number, then pay through 1Link/1Bill (any banking app or e-wallet). Then click "Verify Payment" and the status changes to "Submitted".
- If the status stays "Pending Payment", try another banking app or e-wallet before the deadline. Consumer Numbers expire at the registration closing date and time.
- Payment problems must be reported at least 3 days before the deadline. Complaints on the last day are not entertained. Help: 051-90408184, onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk.
- Incomplete or draft forms do not count as registered, and a successful payment alone does not confirm registration: check the application status — [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22)

**Fee (UNVERIFIED: no official source found)**
- The HEC FAQ describes how to pay but gives no amount — [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)
- The ilmkidunya and Eduvision 2026 articles also give no amount ("check the payable amount on your invoice") — [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22); [Eduvision](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/hec-usat-2026-dates-syllabus-and-how-to-apply-news-3613)
- Third-party figures conflict:
  - Rs 2,000 — [pakadmissions](https://www.pakadmissions.com/hec-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2026-usat)
  - Rs 1,200 (USAT-M guide; also the 2021 launch fee) — [pakadmissions USAT-M guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-m-entry-test-complete-guide); [Nearpeer, Sept 2021](https://nearpeer.org/blog/general/the-universities-of-punjab-say-nope-to-hec-s-aptitude-test)
  - Rs 1,750 — summarised from search results, source page not opened
  - One March 2026 blog said "no registration fee", which looks like an error — [travel-culture blog](https://blog.travel-culture.com/2026/03/16/hec-usat-2026-admission-test-details-for-pakistani-universities)
- Fees are non-refundable and non-transferable (third-party, consistent across sources) — [ilmkidunya USAT hub](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/usat/)

**Roll-number slip**
- **[official]** Candidates must bring a printed Roll Number Slip — [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)
- **[official]** The calendar gives a roll-number-slip issue date for each session, about 9 days before the test — [HEC Test Calendar 2026 (rev. 25 Aug 2026), PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/Test%20Calendar%20for%20Year%202026%20-%20Revsied%20on%2025.08.2026.pdf)

  4. 06.09.2026 / 02.10.2026 / 16.10.2026 / **Sun 25.10.2026** / 16.11.2026 (revised)

### Inferences
- The guide should present dates as "about 5 sessions a year, always on a Sunday; registration closes about 3 weeks before the test; the slip is out about 9 days before; results come 3–6 weeks after". It should link to the official calendar instead of hard-coding dates, because ETC reschedules (Oct 2025, Aug 2026).
- The fee should read "shown on your portal invoice (reported as about Rs 2,000 in 2025-26)", not a fixed number.

### Gaps
- No official fee amount. The ETC portal needs a login, and no official notice found gives it.
- No official list of test cities.
- Whether a candidate can register for more than one category in the same session: not found.
- Whether A-level candidates need IBCC equivalence before taking USAT: not found.

## 2. Test-day rules

### Takeaway
The official HEC FAQ is clear. Bring the printed roll-number slip, an original ID (CNIC/passport, or a B-Form plus a photo result card if under 18), a transparent clipboard, and blue or black ball-point pens. **Calculators are prohibited**, as are phones, smartwatches, earbuds, vapes and all other electronics. A banned item is confiscated and an unfair-means case is registered.

### Cited Findings (all **[official]** from the [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx) unless noted)
- Bring a printed Roll Number Slip and an original valid CNIC or passport.
- Expired CNIC or passport: allowed only with the NADRA slip plus the original expired document.
- Under 18: original B-Form plus an original Matric or Intermediate result card with a photo.
- Lost CNIC: a police report, a NADRA receipt for the new CNIC application, and an original Matric or Intermediate certificate or result card with a photo.
- Photocopies and digital images of ID are not accepted.
- Bring a transparent clipboard and blue or black ball-point pens. Lead pencils, fountain pens and markers are not permitted.
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, **calculators**, earbuds, vapes and other electronic gadgets are prohibited. Any found are confiscated and an unfair-means case is registered.
- A CNIC that differs from the registered one by even one digit, or someone else's CNIC, means the candidate is not allowed to sit the test.
- Electronic communication, recording and imaging devices are prohibited inside centres. Candidates must follow ETC conduct rules — [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22)

### Inferences
- Since calculators are banned, the app's quantitative practice should be designed for mental and paper arithmetic, with no on-screen calculator in mock mode.
- The ban on pencils suggests an OMR answer sheet marked in ink. This is inferred, not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Reporting time: not on the FAQ. It is likely printed on the roll-number slip, but I found no source.
- Dress code: no official source found.
- Whether the test is paper-based or computer-based: not stated officially in what I read.
- Negative marking: not covered here; check pattern research.

## 3. Universities, programmes, scholarships, merit weight and validity

### Takeaway
USAT is **optional** for universities. HEC recommends it, but each university decides whether to accept it and how much weight to give it. Major Punjab universities (PU, GCU) refused it when it launched in 2021. Its clearest uses are (a) **HEC-administered foreign undergraduate scholarships**, where nomination is purely on USAT score (Hungary, Cuba), and (b) some universities that accept it in place of their own test, e.g. NDU Islamabad at 50%+. Validity conflicts between sources: HEC scholarship pages say **1 year** for USAT, some blogs say 2.

### Cited Findings
- USAT began in 2021 as "mandatory" for BS admission. The University of the Punjab and GCU Lahore refused it. PU's registrar said "the universities do not need to follow HEC testing council's USAT", and it became effectively optional — [Nearpeer, 2 Sept 2021](https://nearpeer.org/blog/general/the-universities-of-punjab-say-nope-to-hec-s-aptitude-test)
- Each university decides whether to adopt USAT or run its own test, and sets its own weight for USAT score, Intermediate marks and other requirements. Qualifying marks do not guarantee admission — summarised from search results across [pakadmissions](https://www.pakadmissions.com/higher-education-commission-hec-usat-test-2026) and similar third-party sites (secondary; no official HEC weight policy found)
- NDU Islamabad (Spring 2026 instructions, Nov 2025): "USAT conducted by Education Testing Council (ETC) with 50% score will be accepted in BS Programmes at NDU". Otherwise NDU runs its own CBT entry test (Rs 2,000) — [NDU Admission Instructions Spring 2026 (PDF)](https://ndu.edu.pk/fcs/admissions/Spring-2026/Admission-Instructions-Spring-2026.pdf)
- Results are used as "a standardized admission criterion for several universities, particularly those following HEC guidelines" — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/08/19/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2025/)
- **[official] Stipendium Hungaricum 2026 (Hungary):** applicants need a valid HAT or USAT score with at least 50%. Test validity is 2 years for HAT and 1 year for USAT. TPF Hungary makes the final award — [HEC SHSP eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx); [ProPakistani, 6 Dec 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/06/hec-announces-usat-and-hat-tests-for-stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship-2026/)
- **[official] Government of Cuba undergraduate scholarships 2026-27:** nomination is based on USAT score, open merit. There is no separate test. Scores must be dated on or after 31 Mar 2025. The 10 medical seats use the USAT-M score only — [HEC CGS eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGS/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)
- HEC's own scholarship pages say nomination is based on the USAT/HAT test score (reported in search results) — [HEC AZS eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/AZS/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)
- Validity conflict:
  - 1 year — [t4tutorials](https://t4tutorials.pk/?p=4704); [HEC SHSP](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)
  - 2 years — reported by [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/?p=262031) and [PhoneWorld](https://www.phoneworld.com.pk/all-you-need-to-know-about-hec-etc-testing-services-usat-lat-hat/) (search summaries, not opened)
- Qualifying or passing score reported as 50/100 — third-party sites in search results (e.g. [ilmkidunya USAT result page](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/results/usat-result.aspx)). It matches the 50% minimums used by NDU and HEC SHSP.
- The 2026 calendar was revised to align with the Commonwealth Scholarship Portal, which suggests USAT/HAT scores feed HEC-managed Commonwealth nominations — [HEC Test Calendar 2026 PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/Test%20Calendar%20for%20Year%202026%20-%20Revsied%20on%2025.08.2026.pdf). No page confirming USAT specifically for Commonwealth undergraduate awards was found.

### Inferences
- The app's selling point should be "USAT for HEC foreign scholarships and for universities that accept it". It should not claim USAT is required for all admissions, because that is false. The guide should tell students to check each target university's admission page.
- For guide copy, "valid 1 year" is the safer statement, citing HEC scholarship rules. Individual universities may set their own window.

### Gaps
- No authoritative, current list of universities that accept USAT. Only NDU was confirmed from a primary document.
- No list of merit-weight percentages by university.
- No official HEC statement on general result validity, beyond the scholarship-specific rules.
- MDCAT and ECAT relationship: USAT-M does not appear to replace MDCAT for medical colleges, but no source was found confirming this.

- Gaps a new app can fill:

  - calculator-free quant practice
  - an official-calendar-based date guide
  - ID/test-day checklist
