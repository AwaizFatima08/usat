# USAT (HEC / Education Testing Council) — official structure and scoring, 2025-2026

Research date: 17 Nov 2026. The primary source is HEC's "Content Weightages for HEC Undergraduate Studies Admission/Aptitude Test (USAT)" PDF, 6 pages, one per category. Two copies exist on hec.gov.pk. Copy 1 (ETC folder) has PDF metadata created/modified 10 Aug 2022. Copy 2 (LAO folder, title changed to "Admission/Aptitude Test") was created 7 Nov 2022 and modified 31 Aug 2023. The two copies give the same numbers. A 7 Oct 2026 news guide (pakera.pk) cites ETC's content-weightage page as current for the Oct 2026 sitting and reproduces the same numbers. The etc.hec.gov.pk pages themselves would not load for this research (404, or an unrelated title only).

## Which categories exist now?

### Takeaway
There are six categories, unchanged from 2022 through the Dec 2026 sitting: USAT-E (Pre-Engineering), USAT-M (Pre-Medical), USAT-A (Arts & Humanities), USAT-CS (Computer Science), USAT-GS (General Science) and USAT-COM (Commerce). No category has been added or renamed.

### Cited Findings
- The official PDF lists six categories, "Having 12 years/equivalent Education in": 01 USAT-E Pre-Engineering, 02 USAT-M Pre-Medical, 03 USAT-A Arts & Humanities, 04 USAT-CS Computer Science, 05 USAT-GS General Science, 06 USAT-COM Commerce — [HEC Content Weightages PDF (2022)](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf); [HEC Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT PDF (rev. Aug 2023)](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/Documents/Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT.pdf)
- The HEC ETC FAQ says USAT has six categories. The candidate chooses one by academic stream, and the choice cannot be changed after submission — [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)
- The Dec 2026 sitting (test 13 Dec 2026, registration 8–22 Nov 2026) lists the same six: USAT-A, USAT-COM, USAT-CS, USAT-E, USAT-GS, USAT-M — [ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/)
- A 2022 article lists "USAT-L: Pre Engineering". This is probably a typo for USAT-E, because every official document says USAT-E — [ProPakistani, 25 Jul 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/)
- EduVision writes "USAT-ARTS" for USAT-A, which is informal naming — [EduVision, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/hec-usat-2026-dates-syllabus-and-how-to-apply-news-3613)
- One third-party guide claims there are "seven (07) types". No official source supports this — [studyandexam.com (undated)](https://studyandexam.com/hec-usat-bs-admission.html)

### Inferences
- An app should offer exactly six category tracks, using the official codes: E, M, A, CS, GS, COM.

### Gaps
- The live etc.hec.gov.pk category page could not be fetched, so the 2026 list rests on HEC's FAQ and news reports of the 2026 announcements.

## Test pattern per category (sections, MCQs, marks, time, negative marking, essay)

### Takeaway
All six categories follow the same layout. Part I is 75 MCQs in 100 minutes: Verbal Reasoning 20, Quantitative Reasoning 25 and a 30-MCQ subject block that differs by category. Part II takes 40 minutes and has two essays, each written in English or Urdu: an argument essay (choose 1 of 3, 15 marks) and a narrative essay (choose 1 of 3, 10 marks). The total is 100 marks. No official document mentions negative marking.

### Cited Findings
**Common to all six categories** (official PDF, both copies) — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)

- The PDF heads the table "100% Weight" but gives no marks per MCQ. It states marks only for the essays (15 + 10) — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)
- The total of 100 marks with a qualifying score of 50 is reported by a 2026 news report and by older guides — [ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/); [pakadmissions USAT-A guide, Sep 2026](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide)
- Negative marking: neither the official PDFs nor the HEC FAQ mention it. Only a third-party guide says "None" — [pakadmissions USAT-A guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide). The pakera 2026 guide says negative marking is not stated — [pakera.pk](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)
- Conflicting third-party figures, all wrong against the official PDF:
  - The pakadmissions USAT-A page gives VR 20 + QR 40 = "75 MCQs", which does not add up. It also gives a subject mix with GK/Pak Studies at 30–35% — [pakadmissions](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide)
  - Some sites claim 100 MCQs in 100 or 120 minutes, e.g. English 20, Logical 20, Quant 20, Physics 10 — [summary of search results incl. studyandexam.com](https://studyandexam.com/hec-usat-bs-admission.html)
  - One site claims 75 MCQs, 100 min, 100 marks — [t4tutorials](https://t4tutorials.pk/hec-announces-usat-test-for-admission-in-universities/?amp=1)
- HEC hosts an official sample paper for USAT-CS with verbal items in analogy style ("Select the lettered pair that best expresses a relationship…"). It is useful as a model for item style — [HEC sample paper USAT-CS](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/SAMPLE%20PAPER-USAT-COMPUTER%20SCIENCE.pdf)

### Inferences
- Total marks are probably 75 MCQs × 1 mark + 25 essay marks = 100. This fits the reported 100-mark total, but no official document states "1 mark per MCQ".
- The official test time is 100 + 40 = 140 minutes. The PDF does not say whether the two parts are separately timed in one sitting, but it gives each part its own time.
- Assume no negative marking only as a soft default. It is unconfirmed, and the app should label it so or make it configurable.
- The essays are scored by human graders. An app can only offer self-rating or a rubric, not an official score.

### Gaps
- No official statement on marks per MCQ, negative marking or the essay rubric.
- No official item-level syllabus beyond the sub-topic weights. The subject blocks are named only by subject, presumably at HSSC level.

## Scoring: how results are reported, qualifying marks, validity, attempts

### Takeaway
The minimum qualifying score is 50/100. Results are downloaded as a result card from the candidate's ETC portal account. Validity is disputed: the newest 2026 reports of the HEC announcement say 2 years, while older and third-party pages say 1 year. There is no stated limit on attempts, and the test runs several times a year.

### Cited Findings
- Qualifying score: "HEC has set the minimum qualifying score for USAT at 50 marks out of 100." Validity: "The test score will remain valid for two years." The score is also usable for HEC undergraduate scholarships. Passing does not guarantee admission, since universities apply their own merit — [ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/); techjuice reports the same 2-year figure — [TechJuice 2026](https://www.techjuice.pk/hec-opens-usat-2026-registration-across-pakistan/)
- Conflict: "valid for one year from the date of declaration" — [pakadmissions USAT-A guide, Sep 2026](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide). "One year from the date of the test" — [gotest.com.pk, updated 7 Jan 2026](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/)
- Reporting: results appear on the ETC portal (login with CNIC). Candidates download a result card with a detailed marks sheet, and scores are out of 100 — [gotest.com.pk](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/)
- Answer keys are published after each sitting, one per booklet colour (Blue, Green, Pink, White, Yellow) — [gotest.com.pk](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/). For the 25 Oct 2026 sitting, answer keys for all six categories were published and the result was due 16 Nov 2026 — [pakera.pk, updated 28 Oct 2026](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)
- Universities use the USAT score in their weightage-based merit formula — [ProPakistani, 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/)
- In 2022, "Students getting minimum 50 marks (50%) will be considered as qualified" — [search summary citing a 2021/22 guide (fsconline)](https://fsconline.info/hec-usat-content-weightages-syllabus-for-all-disciplines/amp)

### Inferences
- An app should report a mark out of 100: MCQ correct count plus a self-rated essay out of 25. Flag 50 as the qualifying line, plus a per-section breakdown that mirrors the official result card.
- Use 2 years in the app's wording, because that is the newest reported HEC figure. Note that it was reported by the press, not read from an ETC document.
- Multiple attempts are possible because sittings run quarterly. No cap was found.

### Gaps
- There is no official ETC document on validity, attempt limits or whether the best or latest score counts. No percentile reporting was found.

## Mode, test centres, frequency of sessions

### Takeaway
USAT is a paper-based OMR test in major cities, held roughly quarterly. The 2026 sittings were 5 Jul, 25 Oct and 13 Dec 2026, and earlier 2026 dates were also reported.

### Cited Findings
- "These tests are conducted quarterly throughout the calendar year." The tests are held in major cities across Pakistan. Candidates pick a test city at registration and cannot change it. The answer sheet is an OMR bubble sheet marked with a black or blue ball pen. The fee is paid through 1Link/1Bill — [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)
- Answers on the OMR sheet must be in pen, and pencil answers are not assessed. Phones and calculators are not allowed — [pakera.pk, 7 Oct 2026](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)
- 2026 sittings:
  - 25 Oct 2026, registration closed 2 Oct — [pakera.pk](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)
  - 13 Dec 2026, registration 8–22 Nov — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/)
  - 5 Jul 2026 (deadline 7 Jun) and 19 Apr 2026 were also reported — search summaries of [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/hec-usat-registration-2026-open--apply-by-november-22) and similar
  - 1 Feb 2026 (special sitting) — [gotest.com.pk](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/)
- 2025: registration closed 9 Sep 2025, test 28 Sep 2025 — search summary of [t4tutorials](https://t4tutorials.pk/?p=4704)
- Fee: Rs 1,200 non-refundable in 2022 — [ProPakistani, 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/). No 2026 amount was found, and one March 2026 blog claims there is no fee, which conflicts with the 1Link payment process — [travel-culture blog](https://blog.travel-culture.com/2026/03/16/hec-usat-2026-admission-test-details-for-pakistani-universities)
- One guide says "computer-based or paper-based depending on session". No official source supports this — [pakadmissions](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide)

### Inferences
- The app's mock should mimic a paper test. Allow free navigation within Part I, show one 100-minute timer for the MCQs and a separate 40-minute timer for the essays. Do not use adaptive or computer-based features.
- Booklet colour versions mean several shuffled forms. The app could shuffle question order per attempt.

### Gaps
- The official ETC 2026 Test Calendar PDF returned 404, so the exact list of 2026 sessions is not confirmed from the primary source. No official list of centres or 2026 fee amount was found.

## Changes 2022 → 2023 → 2024 → 2025 → 2026 (which is current)

### Takeaway
The current pattern is 20 VR + 25 QR + 30 subject MCQs in 100 minutes, plus two essays (15 + 10 marks) in 40 minutes. It has been in force since the Aug 2022 HEC weightage document, was re-issued in Aug 2023, and was cited unchanged for Oct 2026. The earlier 2021 to mid-2022 pattern had no subject block and one essay.

### Cited Findings
- 2021 to Jul 2022 (older pattern): Part 1 MCQs 75 marks, split into Verbal Reasoning 35 and Quantitative Reasoning 40. Part 2 was one descriptive essay, 25 marks, 400–500 words, English or Urdu, described as based on the SAT pattern. The test was 28 Aug 2022 — [ProPakistani, 25 Jul 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/). The same 75-MCQ, 100-min + 40-min essay format appears in 2021 guides — [search summary incl. fsconline 2021](https://fsconline.info/hec-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-usat-2021-hec-etc-test)
- Aug 2022 (new weightage): the 20/25/30 split, sub-topic weights and two essays (argument 15, narrative 10) — [HEC Content Weightages PDF, metadata 10 Aug 2022](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)
- Aug 2023 revision: same numbers, with the title changed to "Undergraduate Studies Admission/Aptitude Test" — [HEC Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT PDF, modified 31 Aug 2023](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/Documents/Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT.pdf)
- 2026: the same pattern is cited for the 25 Oct 2026 sitting from ETC's content-weightage page — [pakera.pk, 7 Oct 2026](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)
- Validity may have changed from 1 year (older guides) to 2 years (Nov 2026 reports) — [ProPakistani 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/) vs [gotest](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/)

### Inferences
- No pattern change was found for 2024 or 2025. The app should mirror the 2022/2023 weightage, which is still current in 2026.

### Gaps
- No HEC press release dated 2024–2026 explicitly confirming the pattern was found, so "unchanged" rests on the 2023 PDF still being hosted and on a 2026 news report citing it. Check the live etc.hec.gov.pk content-weightage page before release.
