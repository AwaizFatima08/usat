# USAT (HEC / ETC) syllabus and question style, 2025-2026

Research date: 17 Nov 2026. Main official source: HEC's "Content Weightages for HEC Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT)" PDF. It is 6 pages, one per category. The PDF metadata says it was created and modified on 10 Aug 2022, author Dr. Safdar A. Butt. HEC still hosts it at the same URL, and I found no newer official weightage document. HEC's answer-key page for the 25 Oct 2026 sitting still lists the same six categories, which suggests the structure has not changed. That is an inference, not a published statement.

## Verbal Reasoning / English: item types and counts

### Takeaway
HEC publishes exact counts. All six categories have the same 20-MCQ Verbal Reasoning section:
- Analogy: 5
- Synonym/Antonym: 4
- Sentence Completion: 6
- Comprehension: 5

The official document lists no error identification, critical reasoning or separate grammar items. Sample-paper evidence suggests grammar is tested through sentence completion.

### Cited Findings
- Part I has 75 MCQs in 100 minutes. Section A, Verbal Reasoning, has 20 MCQs: A1 Analogy 05, A2 Synonym/Antonym 04, A3 Sentence Completion 06, A4 Comprehension 05. This split is identical on all six pages (USAT-E, M, A, CS, GS, COM). — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)
- A sample paper hosted by University of Lahore (UOLCC, PDF created Dec 2023, headed "Entry Test Sample Paper, Pre-Engineering Test") shows one sample per type, each with four options A-D. The types are described here, not copied:
  - Analogy: a two-word stem in the form "X is related to Y as Z is related to ___", with four single-word options. The relationship is a familiar everyday tool and use pairing.
  - Synonym: "Which word is a synonym for '<word>'?", with an easy everyday adjective and one obvious distractor (the antonym) among the options.
  - Sentence completion: a grammar blank at the start of a question sentence. It tests the correct auxiliary verb and tense, with distractors from other auxiliaries and wh-words.
  The paper labels the English section "Verbal Reasoning, Weightage 20%". — [UOLCC sample test, Pre-Engineering](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)
- Secondary guides list the verbal topics as antonyms, synonyms, comprehension, parts of speech, tenses, subject-verb agreement and word usage. This suggests grammar points appear inside sentence-completion items. — [PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide)
- One aggregator says each category also has "Eng, Logical Reasoning" subject components. That **conflicts** with the official PDF, which has no separate Logical Reasoning section in USAT. — [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines); contradicted by [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)

### Inferences
- Practice items should use four options (A-D), be short and single-line, and use mostly SSC/HSSC-level vocabulary. The sample items are easier than GRE or SAT items.
- Sentence completion (6 items) probably mixes grammar blanks (tense, auxiliaries, prepositions, articles) with vocabulary blanks. The single sample is grammar-based.
- Comprehension (5 items) probably comes as one short passage with about 5 questions, or two short passages. The passage length was not published.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement of comprehension passage length, or of the number of passages.
- The official HEC sample papers on hec.gov.pk or etc.hec.gov.pk could not be retrieved. An aggregator says HEC uploaded sample papers for all six categories, but I could not open that page (SSL error). The UOL copy may be adapted from HEC's sample, but this is unconfirmed.
- I found no source on whether antonym and synonym items are both used, or in what ratio.

## Quantitative Reasoning / Maths: topics and level

### Takeaway
The official split is the same for every category. Section B has 25 MCQs:
- Arithmetic: 6
- Algebra and functions: 4
- Geometry: 3
- Equations: 3
- Statistics: 3
- Scenario-based / mental mathematics: 6

The sample items are SSC (grade 9-10) level or below. They cover averages of number sequences, simple linear equations, integer inequalities and basic properties of triangles.

### Cited Findings
- B1 Arithmetic 06, B2 Algebra and functions 04, B3 Geometry 03, B4 Equations 03, B5 Statistics 03, B6 Scenario Based/Mental Mathematics 06, for a total of 25 MCQs in every category. — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)
- The UOL sample shows four quantitative styles: the average of the first n even numbers; a whole number satisfying two inequality conditions, where "no solution" is an option; a conceptual geometry statement about equilateral triangles; and solving a one-step linear equation with fractional and negative answer choices. The section is labelled "Quantitative Reasoning, Weightage 25%". — [UOLCC sample test, Pre-Engineering](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)
- A secondary guide lists the QR syllabus as algebra, arithmetic and geometry/mensuration, which does not fully match the official six-part split. — [PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide)

### Inferences
- Likely traps, judged from the sample's option design:
  - Sign errors (a positive fraction offered against the correct negative one).
  - Inverted fractions (a/b offered against b/a).
  - "No solution" for contradictory conditions.
  - "B and C" or "None of these" compound options.
- "Scenario based / mental mathematics" probably means short word problems: percentages, profit and loss, ratio, speed-distance-time, work, and money. Answers should be quick to reach without a calculator. Whether calculators are allowed was not confirmed.
- Statistics items probably cover mean, median, mode and range at SSC level.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement on calculator use, or on whether QR is pitched at SSC or HSSC level. The sample suggests SSC.

## Subject sections per category

### Takeaway
Every category has a 30-MCQ subject block in three parts. The official splits are:

| Category | Section C | Section D | Section E |
|---|---|---|---|
| USAT-E (Pre-Engineering) | Physics 10 | Chemistry 10 | Mathematics 10 |
| USAT-M (Pre-Medical) | Physics 8 | Chemistry 8 | Biology 14 |
| USAT-CS (Computer Science) | Physics 8 | Computer Science 14 | Mathematics 8 |
| USAT-GS (General Science) | Mathematics 10 | Statistics 10 | Economics 10 |
| USAT-COM (Commerce) | Accounting 10 | Commerce 10 | Economics 10 |
| USAT-A (Arts & Humanities) | Islamiat/Ethics 10 | Pakistan Studies 10 | General Knowledge 10 |

HEC publishes no chapter-level topic list. The topic lists below come from secondary sources that follow the intermediate (HSSC / FSc / ICS / ICom / FA) curriculum.

### Cited Findings
- The splits above come from the official PDF. — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf); repeated identically in [StudyAndExam](https://studyandexam.com/hec-usat-bs-admission.html)
- **Conflict on USAT-GS.** Officially it is Mathematics, Statistics and Economics (the Pakistani "General Science" intermediate group, e.g. Maths-Stats-Economics combinations). One aggregator instead lists Biology, Chemistry, Physics and "General Science" topics. Treat the aggregator as wrong. — [HEC PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf) vs [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines)
- Secondary guides say the syllabus follows the relevant intermediate (HSSC) curriculum. No board is named. Eligibility is 12 years of education in the relevant group, and A-Level candidates need IBCC equivalence. — [PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide); [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines)

#### Secondary topic lists

All of these come from [PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide) and [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines). They match FSc chapter titles, but are not official.

**Physics:**
- Measurements; vectors and equilibrium; motion and force; work and energy; rotational and circular motion
- Fluid dynamics; oscillations; waves; physical and geometrical optics
- Electrostatics; current electricity; electromagnetism and induction; alternating current; electronics
- Dawn of modern physics; atomic spectra; nuclear physics
- Thermodynamics is in the all-disciplines list.

**Chemistry:**
- Basic concepts; gases, liquids and solids; chemical bonding
- Chemical equilibrium; reaction kinetics; thermochemistry; electrochemistry
- s-block elements
- Organic fundamentals; aliphatic and aromatic hydrocarbons; alkyl halides; alcohols and phenols; aldehydes and ketones; carboxylic acids
- Macromolecules; Pakistani chemical industries; experimental techniques

**Mathematics (USAT-E, USAT-CS):**
- Number systems; sets, functions and groups; matrices and determinants
- Quadratic equations; partial fractions; sequences and series; induction and binomial theorem
- Permutation, combination and probability; trigonometry
- Functions and limits; differentiation; integration; differential equations
- Coordinate geometry; 3D analytical geometry; vectors

**Biology (USAT-M, 14 items):** cell structure, bioenergetics, genetics, evolution, biotechnology, ecosystems and human physiology. This is a broad list; FSc Biology chapters are implied.

**Computer Science (USAT-CS, 14 items):** programming fundamentals, data representation, control structures, arrays, strings, functions, operating systems, computer networks, Boolean algebra and number systems.

**Accounting (USAT-COM):** accounting principles, journal entries, ledgers, trial balance and financial statements.

**Commerce (USAT-COM):** business organisation, trade, banking and entrepreneurship. The PakAdmissions quiz bank also has "advertising and sales promotion".

**Economics (USAT-COM, USAT-GS):** demand and supply, market structures, national income, inflation and monetary policy.

**Islamiat (USAT-A):** basic Islamic knowledge, Quran, Hadith and Seerah. The official label is "Islamiat/Ethics", so non-Muslim candidates presumably take Ethics. That is an inference from the label.

**Pakistan Studies (USAT-A):** history, culture, geography and politics.

**General Knowledge (USAT-A):** current affairs, history, geography, and science and technology.

- The UOL sample's subject items are conceptual one-liners with four options:
  - Physics: what a net force produces, with a compound "B and C" option.
  - Chemistry: which biomolecule is the cell's information and control centre.
  - Maths: an item that is actually physics, about converting sliding friction to rolling friction.
  The mislabelled maths item shows the sample's low editorial quality. — [UOLCC sample test, Pre-Engineering](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)

### Inferences
- Subject items appear to be recall and concept MCQs at the low end of HSSC difficulty, with few multi-step numericals. This rests on a single sample, so confidence is low.
- With only 8-14 items per subject, items probably sample widely across chapters rather than going deep into any one.
- The USAT-GS subject block is Maths, Stats and Economics, so the general QR statistics items (3) and the GS Statistics section (10) overlap in content.

### Gaps
- There is no official chapter list, no named textbook board (FBISE, Punjab, Sindh, KP, AKU-EB), and no official topic list for Statistics (USAT-GS) or General Knowledge.
- I could not confirm whether GK covers current affairs from a specific period.

## Essay / writing section; Islamiat, Pakistan Studies, GK

### Takeaway
Yes, every category has an essay section. Part II is 40 minutes:
- One argument-based essay, chosen from 3 prompts: 15 marks.
- One narrative-based essay, chosen from 3 prompts: 10 marks.
- Either essay can be written in English or Urdu.

Islamiat/Ethics, Pakistan Studies and GK (10 MCQs each) appear only in USAT-A.

### Cited Findings
- Part II, 40 minutes: "Argument Based Essay Writing Skills 01 out of 03, 15 Marks" and "Narrative Based Essay Writing Skills 01 out of 03, 10 Marks", either in English or Urdu. This is on all six category pages. — [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)
- The UOL sample gives Part I a 75% weightage and Part II a 25% weightage (40 minutes). — [UOLCC sample](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)
- Secondary sources put the total at 100 and the pass mark at 50/100, with no negative marking, a one-year validity, and 400-500 words per essay. — [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines); [PakAdmissions USAT-E](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide)
- **Conflict:** one page marks each essay at 15 (a total of 30), against the 15 + 10 split elsewhere. The same page also says the test "generally consists of 100 MCQ ... may vary from 75 to 100". The official PDF says 75 MCQs and 15 + 10. — [StudyAndExam](https://studyandexam.com/hec-usat-bs-admission.html)
- Islamiat/Ethics, Pakistan Studies and General Knowledge each have 10 MCQs in USAT-A only. — [HEC PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)

### Inferences
- An app should offer essay practice with timed prompts: an argument prompt (take a position and defend it) and a narrative prompt (recount or describe an experience). It should include rubric-based self-rating, since no official rubric was found.

### Gaps
- I found no official marking rubric for the essays, and no official word limit. The 400-500 words figure is secondary only.
- I could not confirm from candidates whether the essay is still administered in every 2025-26 session. Searches returned nothing on this.

## Difficulty, traps, sample paper format

### Takeaway
The format is 4-option MCQs (A-D) on an OMR booklet. Each sitting has five booklet versions (colour codes Blue, Green, Pink, White, Yellow). There is no negative marking, according to secondary sources. The difficulty, judging from the samples, is easy to moderate: SSC-level reasoning and low-HSSC subject recall.

### Cited Findings
- The answer keys for the 25 Oct 2026 USAT (ref F-26-1263) are published per category, in five "book colours" (Blue, Green, Pink, White, Yellow), for all six categories. That means a paper-based test with shuffled booklet versions. — [HEC Answer Keys USAT page](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Answer-Keys-USAT.aspx)
- The test may be computer-based or paper-based depending on the session (secondary). The fee is PKR 1,200 (secondary). — [PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines)
- Reported 2026 sittings include 19 Apr 2026 and 5 Jul 2026, plus the 25 Oct 2026 sitting with published keys. The dates come from blogs and are not verified on etc.hec.gov.pk. — [travel-culture blog, 16 Mar 2026](https://blog.travel-culture.com/2026/03/16/hec-usat-2026-admission-test-details-for-pakistani-universities); [PakAdmissions USAT-E](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide); [HEC Answer Keys](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Answer-Keys-USAT.aspx)
- The sample paper layout is: Part I with weightage and time, one sample item per subject under section headings showing each section's weight, then Part II listing the two essay types without prompts. — [UOLCC sample](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)

### Inferences
- Likely traps, judged from the sample items:
  - Compound options ("B and C", "None of these").
  - Antonyms placed as distractors in synonym items.
  - Sign and reciprocal errors in equations.
  - "No solution" options.
  - Near-miss grammar auxiliaries (was/is/did).
- There is no negative marking, so guessing is free. App guidance should advise answering every item. That is about 1.33 minutes per MCQ (100 min / 75).

### Gaps
- I found no official difficulty statement and no published score statistics.
- I could not open the official HEC sample papers, so the format evidence rests on the UOL-hosted copy. It is consistent with the official weightages, though one of its items is mislabelled.
