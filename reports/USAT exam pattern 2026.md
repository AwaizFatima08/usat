# USAT keeps its 2022 blueprint through 2026

The current HEC USAT, run by HEC's Education Testing Council (ETC), has **six categories (USAT-E, -M, -A, -CS, -GS, -COM)**. All six share one layout. **Part I is 75 four-option MCQs in 100 minutes**: **20 Verbal Reasoning**, **25 Quantitative Reasoning** and a **30-MCQ subject block** that changes by category. **Part II is 40 minutes of writing**: one argument essay (choose 1 of 3, **15 marks**) and one narrative essay (choose 1 of 3, **10 marks**), each written in English or Urdu. The total is **100 marks** and the qualifying line is **50**. This blueprint dates from HEC's August 2022 content-weightage document. HEC re-issued it unchanged in August 2023, and guides for the October 2026 sitting cite it unchanged. No change in 2024, 2025 or 2026 was found. Three gaps matter most for an app. **No official document states negative marking** (secondary sources say none). Marks per MCQ are not stated (1 each is implied). Score validity is disputed: **HEC's scholarship rules say 1 year**, while November 2026 press reports say 2 years. USAT is a **paper-based OMR test, about five times a year, always on a Sunday**, and **calculators are officially banned**. Universities are not required to use it. Its firmest use is HEC's foreign undergraduate scholarships. A Google Play search found **no dedicated USAT app**, only generic Pakistani MCQ banks. That leaves a real opening for an app that mirrors the official blueprint exactly.

## Six categories share a 75-MCQ, 140-minute frame

HEC's weightage PDF has one page per category. The categories are for candidates "having 12 years/equivalent education" in Pre-Engineering (USAT-E), Pre-Medical (USAT-M), Arts & Humanities (USAT-A), Computer Science (USAT-CS), General Science (USAT-GS) and Commerce (USAT-COM) ([HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)). The August 2023 revision, retitled "Admission/Aptitude Test", repeats every number ([HEC Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/Documents/Test-Pattern-USAT-HAT.pdf)). The December 2026 registration still lists the same six codes ([ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/)). Candidates pick one category by academic stream, and **the choice cannot be changed after submission** ([HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)). A seventh category, "USAT-L", and a separate "Logical Reasoning" section both appear only in third-party pages. Treat both as errors.

The common core is fixed down to sub-topic level. **Verbal Reasoning (20)** splits into Analogy 5, Synonym/Antonym 4, Sentence Completion 6 and Comprehension 5. **Quantitative Reasoning (25)** splits into Arithmetic 6, Algebra and functions 4, Geometry 3, Equations 3, Statistics 3 and Scenario-based/Mental Mathematics 6 ([HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)). The 30-item subject block is the only part that differs:

| Category | Section C | Section D | Section E |
|---|---|---|---|
| USAT-E Pre-Engineering | Physics 10 | Chemistry 10 | Mathematics 10 |
| USAT-M Pre-Medical | Physics 8 | Chemistry 8 | Biology 14 |
| USAT-CS Computer Science | Physics 8 | Computer Science 14 | Mathematics 8 |
| USAT-GS General Science | Mathematics 10 | Statistics 10 | Economics 10 |
| USAT-COM Commerce | Accounting 10 | Commerce 10 | Economics 10 |
| USAT-A Arts & Humanities | Islamiat/Ethics 10 | Pakistan Studies 10 | General Knowledge 10 |

Source: [HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf), reproduced for the October 2026 sitting by [pakera.pk](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/).

USAT-GS trips up third-party guides. Officially it is **Maths, Statistics and Economics**, matching Pakistan's "General Science" intermediate group. It is not Physics, Chemistry and Biology, as one aggregator claims ([PakAdmissions all-disciplines guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-guide-entry-tests-for-all-disciplines)). The "Islamiat/Ethics" label suggests non-Muslim candidates take Ethics. That is an inference from the label, not a stated rule. The pattern before August 2022 was different: 75 MCQs split 35 verbal and 40 quantitative, no subject block, and one 25-mark essay ([ProPakistani, 25 Jul 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/)). Many blogs still mix the two patterns, which explains the "100 MCQs" and "VR 20 + QR 40" figures seen online.

## Two essays carry a quarter of the marks, and the scoring has gaps

Part II gives the writing section **25% of the paper**. Candidates write one argument-based essay (choose 1 of 3, 15 marks) and one narrative essay (choose 1 of 3, 10 marks) in 40 minutes, in English or Urdu ([HEC Content Weightages PDF](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/Content%20Weightages%20for%20(USAT).pdf)). That is about 20 minutes per essay. The often-quoted 400–500 words comes from secondary sources and belongs to the old single-essay format ([ProPakistani, 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/)). No official word limit or marking rubric was found.

The 100-mark total and the **50-mark qualifying score** are reported for the December 2026 sitting ([ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/)). They agree with the 50% minimum in HEC's scholarship rules ([HEC SHSP eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)). The PDF heads its table "100% Weight" but gives marks only for the essays. The model that fits all the evidence is therefore **75 MCQs at 1 mark each plus 25 essay marks**. That model is inferred, not published.

**Negative marking is not mentioned in any official document.** One guide says "None" ([pakadmissions USAT-A guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-a-entry-test-complete-guide)). Another, written for October 2026, says it is "not stated" ([pakera.pk](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)). An app should default to no negative marking, label that default as unconfirmed, and make it a setting.

Results come as a result card with a marks breakdown on the candidate's ETC portal ([gotest.com.pk](https://gotest.com.pk/results/hec-usat-test-result/)). HEC publishes answer keys per category in **five booklet colours: Blue, Green, Pink, White and Yellow**. That confirms shuffled paper forms ([HEC Answer Keys USAT](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Answer-Keys-USAT.aspx)). Validity is contested. HEC's own Hungary scholarship page says **USAT is valid for 1 year** (HAT for 2) ([HEC SHSP eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)). The November 2026 press coverage says two years ([ProPakistani, 11 Nov 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/11/11/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2026-across-pakistan/); [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/hec-opens-usat-2026-registration-across-pakistan/)). The HEC primary source should win. App copy should say "1 year for HEC scholarships; check each university". No limit on attempts was found, and with five sittings a year retakes are clearly possible.

## Items sit at SSC to low-HSSC level, with predictable traps

HEC publishes weights but **no chapter-level syllabus**. The best evidence of item style is a Pre-Engineering sample paper hosted by the University of Lahore. It follows the official section weights and uses short, single-line, four-option items ([UOLCC sample test](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)). HEC's own USAT-CS sample uses the classic analogy stem asking for the lettered pair that best expresses a relationship ([HEC sample paper USAT-CS](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/SAMPLE%20PAPER-USAT-COMPUTER%20SCIENCE.pdf)).

In the verbal samples, the synonym item uses everyday words and places the antonym among the distractors. The sentence-completion item tests auxiliaries and tense rather than rare vocabulary. Grammar points such as tenses, subject-verb agreement and word usage therefore probably live inside the six sentence-completion items ([PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide)). The quantitative samples are **SSC-level or below**: averages of number sequences, an integer that must meet two inequalities (with "no solution" as an option), triangle properties, and a one-step linear equation whose distractors flip the sign or invert the fraction ([UOLCC sample test](https://uolcc.edu.pk/media/downloads/sample-test-pre-engineering.pdf)). The official FAQ **bans calculators** ([HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)). The six "mental mathematics" items should therefore be quick percentage, ratio, speed and money problems that can be solved on paper.

Subject items in the sample are conceptual recall one-liners at the low end of HSSC, often with compound "B and C" or "None of these" options. Only 8–14 items cover each subject, so they must sample broadly across FSc, ICS and ICom chapters rather than go deep. Secondary topic lists follow intermediate chapter titles: Physics from measurements to nuclear physics, Chemistry from basic concepts to carboxylic acids, Maths from number systems to differential equations, and CS from programming fundamentals to Boolean algebra ([PakAdmissions USAT-E guide](https://www.pakadmissions.com/blog/hec-etc-usat-e-entry-test-complete-guide)). None of these lists is official, and no textbook board is named. This difficulty picture rests on one sample paper of uneven quality: its "Maths" item is actually physics. Treat it as indicative only.

## Test day runs on paper, ink and an original ID

USAT is an **OMR bubble-sheet test** held in major cities. The candidate chooses a test city at registration and cannot change it later ([HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)). Candidates must bring a printed roll-number slip and an original CNIC or passport. Candidates under 18 bring an original B-Form plus a photo Matric or Intermediate result card. Photocopies and phone images of ID are refused. A CNIC that differs from the registered one by a single digit bars the candidate from the test. Candidates bring a transparent clipboard and **blue or black ball-point pens only**. Lead pencils, fountain pens and markers are not allowed, and pencil answers are not marked ([pakera.pk](https://pakera.pk/hec-usat-2026-test-25-october-guide/)). Phones, smartwatches, calculators, earbuds and vapes are confiscated, and the candidate faces an unfair-means case ([HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)). No reporting time or dress code was found.

Registration is online only, through the ETC portal, under the candidate's own CNIC or B-Form. The name must match the Matric certificate, and duplicate profiles are banned. The fee is paid with a Consumer Number through 1Link/1Bill, and payment problems must be reported at least three days before the deadline ([HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ's.aspx)). HSSC graduates and candidates awaiting results can apply ([ProPakistani, Aug 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/08/19/hec-opens-registration-for-usat-2025/)). **The fee amount is unverified**: blogs quote Rs 2,000 ([pakadmissions](https://www.pakadmissions.com/hec-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2026-usat)), while Rs 1,200 was the 2022 figure ([ProPakistani, 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/07/25/all-you-need-to-know-about-hecs-undergraduate-studies-admission-test-2022/)).

HEC's revised 2026 calendar has **five sittings, all on Sundays**: 1 Feb, 19 Apr, 5 Jul, 25 Oct and 13 Dec. Registration closes 2–4 weeks before each test, roll-number slips come out about 9 days before, and results follow 3–6 weeks after ([HEC Test Calendar 2026, rev. 25 Aug 2026](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/Test%20Calendar%20for%20Year%202026%20-%20Revsied%20on%2025.08.2026.pdf)). ETC moves dates: the October 2025 test slipped to November ([Dawn, 15 Oct 2025](https://www.dawn.com/news/1948957)). An app should link to the calendar rather than hard-code dates.

## Scholarships, not universities, give USAT its weight, and no app owns it

USAT launched in 2021 as "mandatory" for BS admission. The University of the Punjab and GCU Lahore refused it, and it became optional in practice ([Nearpeer, Sept 2021](https://nearpeer.org/blog/general/the-universities-of-punjab-say-nope-to-hec-s-aptitude-test)). Each university now decides whether to accept it and how much weight to give it. Only one current university acceptance was confirmed from a primary document: **NDU Islamabad accepts a 50% USAT score** for BS programmes instead of its own test ([NDU Spring 2026 instructions](https://ndu.edu.pk/fcs/admissions/Spring-2026/Admission-Instructions-Spring-2026.pdf)). The firmest demand comes from HEC-run foreign scholarships. **Hungary's Stipendium Hungaricum** requires a valid USAT or HAT at 50% or more ([HEC SHSP eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)). **Cuba's 2026-27 undergraduate scholarships** nominate purely on USAT score, with the 10 medical seats using USAT-M only ([HEC CGS eligibility](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGS/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx)). The 2026 calendar was also realigned with the Commonwealth Scholarship Portal ([HEC Test Calendar 2026](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Documents/Test%20Calendar%20for%20Year%202026%20-%20Revsied%20on%2025.08.2026.pdf)). That points to wider scholarship use, though no page confirms it for USAT specifically.

On Google Play (Pakistan store), the searches "USAT", "USAT test preparation" and "HEC USAT MCQs" turned up **no USAT-specific app**. They returned generic NTS, PPSC, MDCAT and job-test MCQ banks rated about 3.7–4.8, such as TestPoint, PakMcqs, PakTest and TopGrade.pk ([Play: USAT test preparation](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=USAT%20test%20preparation&c=apps&hl=en&gl=PK); [Play: HEC USAT MCQs](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=HEC%20USAT%20MCQs&c=apps&hl=en&gl=PK)). USAT prep lives instead on web quiz banks ([pakadmissions quizzes](https://www.pakadmissions.com/quiz-listing/hec-etc/usat-cs-entry-test/comp-science/computer-software); [gotest](https://gotest.com.pk/preparations/usat-test-online)), in Dogar print guides and in coaching academies. This scan has limits. The result pages were cut off, install counts and reviews were not collected, and edtech platforms such as Maqsad and Nearpeer were not checked for USAT courses. They are the most likely serious competitors.

## Conclusion

A faithful mirror of the exam is mostly a matter of discipline. The official blueprint is precise down to "3 geometry items", yet almost every third-party guide gets it wrong in some way. That makes accuracy itself the app's strongest selling point. The design follows from the evidence: six fixed category tracks, each mock drawing exactly 5/4/6/5 verbal and 6/4/3/3/3/6 quantitative items plus the category's 30-item block. Part I runs on a 100-minute timer with free navigation across four-option items, and Part II on a separate 40-minute timer with a choice of 3 argument and 3 narrative prompts in English or Urdu. Self-rating the essays against a rubric, out of 15 and 10, is the one feature generic MCQ banks cannot copy. No calculator in mock mode, a score out of 100 with the 50 line marked, and shuffled forms like the five booklet colours complete the mirror.

Three facts are still open and must not be stated as settled: negative marking, marks per MCQ and score validity. Make the first a configurable "none (unconfirmed)" default, and phrase validity from HEC's 1-year scholarship rule. Position the app honestly as "USAT for HEC foreign scholarships and the universities that accept it", not as a universal admission requirement. Before release, re-check the live etc.hec.gov.pk content-weightage page, which could not be loaded during this research. Every structural claim above depends on the 2023 PDF still being current.
