The real pattern, all six categories

Part I: 75 MCQs, 100 minutes

Verbal Reasoning 20 (analogy, synonym and antonym, sentence completion, comprehension), Quantitative Reasoning 25 (arithmetic, algebra, geometry, equations, statistics, scenario and mental maths) and a 30-question subject block, exactly as HEC's content weightages set out.

Part II: two essays, 40 minutes

Choose one argument prompt (15 marks) and one narrative prompt (10 marks) from three each, write against the clock, then rate yourself against marking points and a model answer.

20 mocks per category

USAT-E (Physics, Chemistry, Maths), USAT-M (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), USAT-CS (Physics, Computer Science, Maths), USAT-GS (Maths, Statistics, Economics), USAT-COM (Accounting, Commerce, Economics) and USAT-A (Islamiat or Ethics, Pakistan Studies, General Knowledge).

What you get

  • Real timing. A 100-minute Part I and a 40-minute Part II, with flags and an answer-sheet style question map. No calculator, like the real test.
  • Scores that help. Marks out of 100 against the qualifying score of 50, your result by section, and the topics to work on next.
  • Every answer explained. See the working and why the tempting option is wrong.
  • Study notes and practice. Notes, worked examples and practice sets for every topic in every category, separate from the mocks.
  • Memory tricks. Formula sheets, tables and mnemonics for every subject, and how to make them stick.
  • Guidance and rules. Registration, test-day rules, the OMR sheet, time plans, essay technique and study plans.
  • Private and offline. No account. Your scores stay on your phone.
